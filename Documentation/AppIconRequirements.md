# LiDARStudio App Icon Handoff

Copyright © 2026 Dugi Selmanaj. All rights reserved.

The approved `Neon LiDAR Bust Scanner` artwork supplied by Dugi Selmanaj is configured as the `AppIcon` application icon in Debug and Release. The source was resized to the required 1024 × 1024 RGB PNG without transparency; no generated or placeholder artwork was substituted.

Required source artwork:

- 1024 × 1024 pixels, square, without transparency.
- Premium black or dark graphite background.
- Modern 3D-scanning identity.
- Silver or white geometric object.
- Lime-green or turquoise LiDAR accents.
- Clear silhouette and legibility at small icon sizes.

The app reuses the compiled primary icon on the About screen so the artwork remains single-sourced from `Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset`. Preserve the original master outside derived build products.
